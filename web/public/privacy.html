NEARvest privacy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

NEARvest is a pixel farming game on the NEAR testnet, made by Ardian Reffa for the NEAR Hackathon. It is a test version: there is no real money in the game and testnet tokens and NFTs have no real value. This page says plainly what the game stores and why.

What we store

  • Sign-in with Google: your Google account ID (the "sub" number) and your email address, only to sign you in and to keep one garden per Google account. We never see your Google password.
  • Sign-in with a NEAR wallet: your NEAR account name (it is public anyway). We never receive your wallet's keys.
  • Your garden: the garden name you choose, your NEAR testnet account name, your avatar and your game progress (trees, seeds, fruit, PANEN balance, XP, check-ins, TaniBot settings and its log).
  • Anti-abuse: the IP address used when a garden is created, and a random device ID for "Try as Judge", to limit free welcome gifts per day.
  • Sign-in session: a random token. The server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) copy; your browser keeps the token so you stay signed in for up to 30 days.
  • In your browser only (local storage): your settings (music, sound, menu) and, for wallet gardens, a limited game key that can only spend PANEN in the Shop.

Account keys (custodial, testnet only)

If you sign in with Google, the game creates a NEAR testnet account for you (name.nearvest.testnet) and its server holds that account's key, encrypted (AES-256-GCM). The same is true for TaniBot's own small account. This is a custodial design that we use only on the testnet so that you never need a wallet or pay fees. If you sign in with your own NEAR wallet, your garden lives in your own account and your keys stay with you.

Public blockchain data

Game actions such as planting a tree (a Tree NFT), earning or spending PANEN are NEAR testnet transactions. They are public, show your NEAR account name, and cannot be deleted by us or anyone else.

Who else is involved

  • We do not sell your data, and the game has no ads and no tracking or analytics tools.
  • Google handles the Google sign-in; the page fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • The NEAR testnet network records the transactions.
  • TaniBot's AI helper (NEAR AI Cloud) receives only garden numbers (trees, fruit, prices, budget), never your name or email.
  • The web page is hosted on Vercel and the game server on a rented server (VPS).

How long, and deleting your data

We keep the data while the game runs for the hackathon and its testing. Email us and we will delete your garden and sign-in data from the game server. Records on the public NEAR testnet stay, as explained above.

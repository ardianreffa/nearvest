NEARvest terms of use

Last updated: 8 November 2026

A testnet game for the NEAR Hackathon

NEARvest is a pixel farming game made by Ardian Reffa for the NEAR Hackathon. It runs only on the NEAR testnet. It is a test version, offered free and "as is", without any guarantee. Gardens, balances or the whole game may be reset, changed or stopped at any time.

No real money, no real value

  • $PANEN, Tree NFTs and every other item in the game are testnet assets with no real value. They cannot be bought with real money and are not an investment.
  • The game pays all network fees. The only exception: a NEAR wallet garden pays one tiny testnet fee once, to set up the Shop.

Your account

  • One Google account = one garden. You can also sign in with your own NEAR wallet.
  • With Google sign-in, the game creates and holds your NEAR testnet account's key for you, encrypted (custodial, testnet only). Details: Privacy.
  • Play fair: do not attack the game, abuse the free gifts or try to break other players' gardens. We may remove gardens that do.

Code and artwork

The game's code is released under the MIT license. Some pictures were made with an AI image generator at the author's request; all assets and their licenses are listed in the project's repository.
